# Scholarships for Students

Scholarships for Students is a free website designed to help students discover educational opportunities around the world.

The platform helps students explore:

- Scholarships
- International schools
- Summer programs
- Academic competitions
- University opportunities

## How It Works

Students answer a few simple questions, including:

- Country
- Education level
- Current year of study
- Age

The website then displays opportunities that may be relevant to their profile.

## Features

- Personalized opportunity recommendations
- Save opportunities for later
- Detailed opportunity information
- Mobile-friendly design
- Easy navigation

## Mission

Many students miss opportunities simply because they do not know they exist.

This project aims to make educational opportunities more accessible to students worldwide.
